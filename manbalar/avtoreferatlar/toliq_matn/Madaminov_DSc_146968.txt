qolayotgan bo‘lib, ayniqsa, foiz stavkalarining o‘zgaruvchanligi va geosiyosiy

        Xorijiy ilmiy tadqiqotlarni umumlashtirish asosida quyidagi ilmiy xulosalarni
ajratish mumkin. Birinchidan, moliyaviy barqarorlikni ta’minlashda risk-menejment
tizimi alohida risk turlarini boshqarish bilan cheklanmay, korporativ boshqaruv, risk
madaniyati, ma’lumotlar sifati va shaffoflik bilan integratsiyalashgan bo‘lishi lozim.
Ikkinchidan, zamonaviy metodologiya mikro darajadagi ko‘rsatkichlar bilan birga tizimli
va makroprudensial xatarlarni ham inobatga olishi zarur. Uchinchidan, xorijiy tajriba
bank barqarorligini baholashda panel regressiya, dinamik GMM, z-score, CoVaR, MES,
stress-test, scenario analysis va kompozit indekslardan kompleks foydalanishni
maqsadga muvofiq deb ko‘rsatadi. To‘rtinchidan, raqamlashtirish, iqlim risklari va
nobank sektor bilan aloqadorlik risk-menejment metodologiyasining yangi avlod
komponentlariga aylanmoqda.

     В мировой практике при проведении стресс-тестирования используются

весовой коэффициент в зависимости от его влияния на устойчивость банка.
     В-четвертых, принцип агрегации - все частные индексы объединяются в
единый интегральный рейтинг. Таким образом, качество риск-менеджмента
становится математически измеримым показателем.
     Если Xi - фактическое значение i-го показателя, а Xmin, Xmax - нормативные
пределы, то для положительно влияющих показателей:
                      Si=(Xi−Xmin)/( Xmax−Xmin)×100
для отрицательных (повышающих риск) показателей:
                      Si=(Xmax−Xi)/( Xmax−Xmin)×100
     Si∈[0,100].
     Например:

     доля проблемных кредитов (NPL) - отрицательная;
     частота операционных потерь - отрицательная.

                              ЗАКЛЮЧЕНИЕ

      Несмотря на то, что в последние годы коммерческие банки достигли
положительных результатов в обеспечении финансовой стабильности на
основе совершенствования системы риск-менеджмента, существуют
проблемы и недостатки, ожидающие своего решения, к которым можно
отнести следующие:
      1. Повышение уровня капитализации и ликвидности банковской системы
на пути ее устойчивого развития, совершенствование механизма
кредитования, снижение уровня риска всех категорий. Эта проблема касается
и банков, в том числе коммерческих, осуществляющих деятельность в
Республике Узбекистан. Глубоко изучена деятельность коммерческих банков
развитых стран и определены возможности развития «контрактных банков» в

         INTRODUCTION (abstract of the doctoral (DSc) dissertation)

        The aim of the study is to develop scientific proposals and practical
recommendations for improving the methodology of ensuring financial stability based
on the improvement of the risk management system in commercial banks.
        Research objectives:
        comparative analysis of the development of the legal and regulatory framework
for banking services, improvement based on the implementation of a mechanism for
effective management of the stability of commercial banks' activities using innovative
management methods;
        measurement and monitoring of risks in commercial banks, and improving the
differentiation and diversification of the banking services market;
        studying foreign experience in organizing and ensuring the stability of banking
services, as well as improving the effective management mechanism by expanding off-
balance sheet activities in the republic's banks;
        improving and increasing the efficiency of the mechanism for ensuring the
stability of the branch of the Main Department of the National Bank in Tashkent based
on an innovative approach, as well as developing scenarios for determining its strategic
prospects;
        development of measures for the development and improvement of the system of
commercial banks by increasing the reliability and stability of the mechanism of
interbank relations and their activities.
        The object of the research work is the activities of commercial banks operating
in the Republic of Uzbekistan.
        The subject of the research is organizational-economic and financial relations
related to the improvement of the methodology for ensuring financial stability based on
the improvement of the risk management system in commercial banks.
        Research methods. The dissertation utilizes deductive and inductive methods,
scientific observation, analysis and synthesis, statistical grouping, correlation and
regression, monographic research, social surveys, comparison, and economic-
mathematical methods.
        The scientific novelty of the research is as follows:
        in the formation of a conceptual model of the risk management system aimed at
ensuring financial stability in commercial banks, it is proposed to integrate the processes
of identification, assessment, monitoring, and management of banking risks with asset
and liability management mechanisms, capital adequacy, liquidity indicators, and stress
test results;
        a proposal has been substantiated to improve financial strategies for ensuring the
stability of commercial banks by using the "low" (0-50), "medium" (50-74), and "high"
(75-100) scales of the rating method for assessing the quality of risk management in
commercial banks;
        based on a comprehensive assessment of risks affecting financial stability in
commercial banks, the Risk-based Early Warning System (EWS) risk early warning
system model is proposed;
        in improving the risk management system in commercial banks based on

        taking into account the priority areas of financial strategies for ensuring the
stability of commercial banks in Uzbekistan, forecast scenarios for their development

        The practical results of the study are as follows:
        as a result of applying innovative management mechanisms and technologies to

bank operations and services has made it possible to create scientific recommendations
and practical proposals aimed at reducing risks and increasing efficiency in banking
business and private entrepreneurship; the proposed methodological developments for
ensuring the stability of commercial bank activities have created opportunities for making
decisions on the volume of banking services, interest rates, average and marginal costs,
and financing investment projects through bank loans.

**2. Mazmun**

*4-bob. Cocozza (EBA KRI)*
- Maqsad: EBA tanlagan asosiy risk indikatorlari (KRI) orqali nazoratchining risk xaritasini tiklash, so‘ng bozorning deleveraging va de-risking jarayonlarini qanday baholashini tekshirish (p.105–107).
- EBA KRI bazasi: 53 ta indikator, har chorakda, 20 ta EEA mamlakatidagi 57 ta bank, 2009-yildan boshlab. Banklar har bir milliy sektor aktivlarining kamida 50% ini qamraydi (p.111).
- Table 4.1 (p.112–113) KRIlarni to‘rt blokka ajratadi:
  - kapital yetarliligi (1–3) va kapital talabining tarkibi (4–11);
  - kredit riski va aktivlar sifati (12–21): >90 kun muddati o‘tgan kreditlar, qamrov koeffitsiyenti va boshqalar;
  - rentabellik (22–33): ROE, ROA, cost-income va boshqalar;
  - balans tuzilmasi (34–46): LTD, Tier1/(TA − nomoddiy aktivlar), D/E va boshqalar;
  - mutlaq qiymatlar (47–53).
- Table 4.2 (p.114): KRIlar 4 sohani qamraydi, Risk Dashboard esa 11 ta risk omilini ko‘rsatadi (bozor, operatsion, konsentratsiya, likvidlik, funding, regulyativ, suveren va boshqalar). Xulosa: nazorat asosan aktivlar sifati va rezervlarga qaratilgan.
- Dinamika (p.115–117): D/E 2009-yil 4-chorakdagi 18.71 dan 2014-yil 2-chorakda 16.13 gacha tushgan. RWA/TA 35–39% oralig‘ida. LTD 1.20 dan 1.11–1.13 gacha kamaygan, ya’ni deleveraging kreditlash sekinlashuvi hisobiga bo‘lgan.
- Model (4.1), p.118: TEV = f(RWA/TA, DER, lnTA, ΔGDP, INTM, EQMC, D2007, D2009). Tanlanma: EuroStoxx Banks tarkibidagi 31 bank, 9 mamlakat, 2007–2013, 182 kuzatuv. Bu yevro hududi MFI aktivlarining 46.84% i (p.120–121).
- Natijalar, random-effects GLS (Table 4.8, p.124):
  - RWA/TA 0.415, DER 0.011, NIM/TA −6.23, ΔGDP −1.24, regulyativ dummy −0.079;
  - lnTA ahamiyatsiz;
  - R² within 52.9%.
- Talqin: bozor de-risking (RWA/TA ni kamaytirish)ni deleveragingdan ko‘ra yuqoriroq baholaydi (p.123–125).
- Xulosa (p.125): kredit riskiga haddan tashqari urg‘u boshqa risklarni e’tibordan chetda qoldirish xavfini tug‘diradi. Rezerv orqali kredit riskini nazorat qilish past foyda sharoitida barqaror emas.

*8-bob. Tutino va boshq. (Bazel 3 strategiyalari)*
- Tanlanma (p.212–215): 2011-yilda Tier1 < 11% bo‘lgan 10 ta Italiya bank guruhi. Maqsad 11% = 6% + 2.5% CCB + 2.5% CCyB.
- 2011-yil o‘rtachalari (Table 8.2, p.214): Tier1 8.00%; ΔRWA 6.89%; RWA/TA 64.81%; dividend to‘lash ulushi 39.44%; ROA −0.12%; goodwill hisobga olinmagan ROA 0.30%.
- Model (Morelli 2011; 8.1–8.7, p.216–217): kapital faqat taqsimlanmagan foyda hisobiga o‘sadi, Tier1 barqarorligi sharti qo‘yiladi. Natijada:
  - Tier1* = ROA·(1−d)·(1+g) / (RWA/TA · g), bu yerda g = RWA o‘sishi;
  - shu tenglamadan ROA_stab, RWA/TA_stab, d_stab va g_stab chiqariladi.
- Natijalar (p.218–225):
  - 11% ni barqaror ushlab turish uchun o‘rtacha ROA_stab 1.28% bo‘lishi kerak. Bu amaldagidan +1.40 p.p. (goodwillsiz +0.98 p.p.) yuqori;
  - faqat dividendlarni qisqartirish yetmaydi: d_stab = −48.55%;
  - RWA o‘sishini ~9.7 p.p. qisqartirish kerak bo‘ladi.
- Amalda (Table 8.7, p.226): ROA 2012-yilda 0.03%, 2013-yilda −0.68%; Tier1 8.80% va 8.66%; RWA/TA 57.34% gacha tushgan.
- Tarixiy ROA 2004–2006 yillarda atigi +0.1 p.p. o‘sgan, ya’ni kerakli o‘sishning 10% i (p.227–228, Table 8.8).
- 2012-yilgi so‘rov (p.229): banklarning 65% i kapital oshirishni, 58% i o‘z-o‘zini moliyalashni, 52% i RWA qisqartirishni rejalagan.
- Xulosa (p.231): 11% Tier1 uchun zarur rentabellikka qisqa muddatda erishib bo‘lmaydi. Bir nechta dastakni birga qo‘llash kerak.

*1-bob (qisman). Floreani va boshq.*
- Tanlanma: 59 ta Yevropa bank guruhi, 2005–2011, 413 kuzatuv, ulardan 38 tasi listingda. Bog‘liq o‘zgaruvchi – bank betasi (p.13–14).
- O‘zgaruvchilar (Table 1.2, p.17): IMPL/GL, LLP/GL, RIL/IMPL (qamrov), LLP/PIMOP, RWA/TA, DMMS/TE, ROE.
- FE natijalari (Table 1.5, p.24): CRISIS×LLPGL −80.5***; GL/TA +0.87***.
- Arellano–Bond (Table 1.7, p.27): LLPGL −36.1**; RWATA +56.7*; ROE ahamiyatsiz; Hansen testi o‘tadi.
- Xulosalar (p.29–32): betani rezervlar va RWA/TA belgilaydi, joriy rentabellik emas. Dinamik (ispancha) rezervlash va NPL hamda forbearance ta’riflarini uyg‘unlashtirish tavsiya qilinadi.

**3. Dissertatsiyada qo‘llash**
- §1.3 – Tutino formulasi ("Moslashtiriladigan jadval/formulalar" bo‘limidagi 1-band) orqali O‘zbekiston banklari uchun barqarorlashtiruvchi ROA hisoblanadi. U MBII-Basel blokida "ichki kapital generatsiyasi" signali bo‘ladi: ROA_stab > ROA amaldagi bo‘lsa, ogohlantirish.
- §1.3/2.1 – EBA KRI to‘rt bloki (kapital, aktivlar sifati, rentabellik, balans tuzilmasi) MBIIning beshta blokini xalqaro amaliyat bilan asoslaydi. Valyuta bloki KRIda yo‘qligini MBIIning qo‘shimcha hissasi sifatida ko‘rsatish mumkin.
- §3.1 – Cocozzaning xulosasi blok og‘irliklarini asoslashda ishlatiladi: faqat aktivlar sifatiga urg‘u berish "miyopiya"ga olib keladi. Aktivlar bloki (0.3) dominant bo‘lsa-da, likvidlik va valyuta bloklari saqlanishi kerak.
- §4.1 – ESRB vositalari (CCyB 0–2.5%, LTD, NSFR, SIFI qo‘shimchasi) va "qat’iy baza + diskretsiya" tamoyili tizimli muhim banklar qoidalari va signal chegaralarini qurish uchun.
- §3.3 – Floreanining CRISIS×LLP talqini va beta–RWA/TA bog‘liqligi NPL sun’iy yo‘ldosh modelida rezerv o‘zgaruvchisini kiritishni asoslaydi.

**4. Iqtiboslar**
- "The EBA KRI is an original set of 53 indicators collected on a quarterly basis by national supervisors, from a sample of 57 European banks" (p.111).
- "de-risking and provisioning are only a part of a proper risk-management approach and, in a sense, they are not proper risk management" (p.114).
- "prospective risk management could be not really sustainable risk management" (p.125).
- "[A] policy that uses primarily prudential tools to limit systemic or system-wide financial risk…" (p.183).
- "A solution at some point on the scale between zero discretionality … and full discretionality … appears to be preferable" (p.196).
- "the profitability levels necessary to support a Tier 1 ratio of 11 per cent appear difficult to reach, at least in the short term" (p.231).
- "Current performances are not significant in explaining systematic risk. The magnitude of loan-loss provisions plays the most significant role" (p.32).

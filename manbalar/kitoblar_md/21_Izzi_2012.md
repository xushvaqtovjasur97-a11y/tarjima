# Izzi L., Oricchio G., Vitale L. «Basel III Credit Rating Systems» (Palgrave, 2012)

**Bibliografiya:** Izzi L., Oricchio G., Vitale L. *Basel III Credit Rating Systems: An Applied Guide to Quantitative and Qualitative Models.* – Basingstoke: Palgrave Macmillan, 2012. – xxii + 330 p.

## Ch.1. Kirish: EMH va Basel III (p.1–10)
- Mualliflar samarali bozor gipotezasini (EMH) inkor qiladi. Ular cheklangan ratsionallik va "adaptive market hypothesis" g'oyasini taklif qiladi (p.1–2).
- Banklarning uch biznes-modeli ajratiladi: originate-and-distribute, originate-and-hold, originate-and-manage (p.3).
- Inqiroz natijalari: global yo'qotishlar 771,9 mlrd yevro, kapital oshirish esa 661,9 mlrd yevro bo'lgan (Table 1.1, p.4). "Hard-to-value" aktivlar kapitalning 23–175% ini tashkil etgan (p.4).
- Basel III kapital bo'yicha 4 element kiritadi (p.5–6): kapital sifati va shaffofligi; kontragent riskini qamrab olish; leverage ratio; kontrsiklik buferlar.
- Likvidlik bo'yicha 3 element (p.6): LCR, NSFR va monitoring vositalari.
- Asosiy tezis: rating modelining aniqlik koeffitsienti (AR) qancha yuqori bo'lsa, regulyativ kapital talabi shuncha past bo'ladi (p.6).

## 3.2 Bank rating modeli (p.69–88; metodologiya 3.1 bilan umumiy)
- Bank va suveren uchun "shadow rating" yondashuvi qo'llanadi. Model tashqi reytinglarni takrorlaydi va regressiya tashqi PD larga nisbatan quriladi (Table 3.1, p.69).
- Ma'lumotga talablar (p.69):
  - ishlab chiqish uchun kamida 100 ta reytinglangan kontragent;
  - validatsiya uchun kamida 50 ta;
  - good/bad yondashuvida har bir izohlovchi o'zgaruvchiga kamida 30 ta defolt.
- Agentlik reytingini PD ga o'tkazish shkalasi, 12 daraja (Table 3.2, p.70): 0,01; 0,03; 0,07; 0,12; 0,24; 0,40; 0,70; 1,20; 3,50; 6,10; 10,5; 20,0%.
- Bir nechta tashqi reyting bo'lsa, ularning o'rtachasi olinadi: PD_external = (1/n)·ΣPD_i (p.72). Omillar t yilga, tashqi PD esa t+1 yilga bog'lanadi.
- Sifat savolnomasi 1 (yaxshi) dan 5 (yomon) gacha shkalada baholanadi va har bir savolga yo'riqnoma beriladi (p.73).
- Univariat bosqich: tozalash, outlier larni chiqarish, normallashtirish. Bashorat kuchi "shadow accuracy ratio" bilan o'lchanadi (p.74–75):
  - SCDF_i = SCDF_{i−1} + PD_i/ΣPD_j;
  - RP = A/(A+B);
  - SAR = RP_model / RP_shadow.
- Multivariat bosqich (p.76):
  - ln((1−PD)/PD) = β0 + Σβ_j·x_j, OLS bilan baholanadi;
  - Score = Σα_j·x_j, bu yerda α_j = β_j/Σβ_k va Σα_j = 1 (intersept kiritilmaydi);
  - stepwise ahamiyatlilik darajasi 1–10%.
- Korrelyatsiya qoidasi: ikki omil orasidagi korrelyatsiya har bir omilning bog'liq o'zgaruvchi bilan korrelyatsiyasidan kichik bo'lishi kerak (p.77).
- Kalibrlash logit yoki eksponensial egri chiziq bilan bajariladi. Model sinfi tashqi reytingdan 1–2 notch dan ortiq farq qilmasligi kerak (p.77).
- Bank uchun omillar ro'yxati (Table 3.6, p.81–82):
  - Kapitallashuv: Tier 1 ratio, Total capital ratio, Equity/Assets, Equity/Loans, ichki kapital o'sishi;
  - Funding & liquidity: Liquid assets/TA, Liquid assets/(ST+customer funding), Interbank funding/Total funding, Customer funds/Loans va boshqalar;
  - Rentabellik: ROA, ROE, Cost/Income, NII/ATA, PBT/ATA va boshqalar;
  - Aktivlar sifati: NPL/Gross loans, LLR/NPL, (NPL−LLP)/Equity, LLP/Operating income, kredit o'sishi;
  - Hajm: Total assets, Equity, Tier 1.
- Sifat omillari (Table 3.7, p.83): mamlakat (regulyatsiya, zaxira yaratish tartibi, sektor konsentratsiyasi); menejment (strategiya, risk-menejment, shaffoflik); biznes (diversifikatsiya, bozor pozitsiyasi); funding (depozit barqarorligi, FX likvidlik); bozor va kredit riski.
- Rivojlangan mamlakat banki modeli (Table 3.8, p.84): miqdoriy modul 70%, sifat moduli 30%.
  - Miqdoriy modul ichida: Tier 1 – 13; Liquid assets/TA – 10; Cost/Income – 20; LLP/Operating income – 22; Total equity – 35.
  - Sifat moduli ichida: Transparency – 35; Risk management – 22; Asset quality – 14; Market risk – 10; Regulation – 6; Market position – 5; Strategy – 4; Org. structure – 4.
- Rivojlanayotgan mamlakat banki modeli (Table 3.9, p.85): miqdoriy modul 80%, sifat moduli 20%.
  - Miqdoriy modul ichida: Equity/TA – 10; Net interest expense/Avg funding – 20; Overheads/ATA – 22; Loan growth – 8; LLR/Gross loans – 12; Total assets – 28.
  - Sifat moduli ichida: FX liquidity – 30; Mgmt stability – 15; Asset quality – 15; Sector concentration – 10; Risk management – 10; qolganlari 5 dan.
- Ona kompaniya qo'llovi (Fig. 3.3, Table 3.10, p.80–86): PD_adj = w·PD_Parent + (1−w)·PD_Bank.
  - Support matritsasi: to'liq kafolatda w = 95–100%; kovenantlar bo'lsa 0–80%; yozma majburiyat bo'lmasa 0–60%.
  - Penalty matritsasi (ona bank zaif bo'lsa): w = 0–100%.
- Davlat qo'llovi (Table 3.11, Fig. 3.4, p.87–88):
  - davlat ulushi >50% yoki to'liq kafolat bo'lsa w = 100%;
  - implitsit qo'llov bankning hajm reytingiga bog'liq: Top-5 uchun 80/50%, Top-6–10 uchun 70/40%, Top-11–20 uchun 50/30%, Top-21–30 uchun 30/15% (mos ravishda "juda qo'llab-quvvatlovchi" va "o'rtacha" muhit).
  - Ham ona bank, ham davlat qo'llovi bo'lsa, yakuniy PD sifatida tuzatilgan PD larning min, o'rtacha yoki max qiymati olinadi (p.84).

## Ch.6 Validatsiya (p.114–146)
- Validatsiya kamida yiliga bir marta o'tkaziladi. Hayot sikli: boshlang'ich validatsiya, monitoring (yil ichida), to'liq qayta validatsiya (Fig. 6.1, p.114–115).
- Uch soha tekshiriladi: model, jarayon va IT tizimi (Fig. 6.2, p.116).
- Master shkala: defoltsiz qarzdorlar uchun kamida 7 daraja va defolt uchun 1 daraja; bitta darajada ortiqcha konsentratsiya bo'lmasligi kerak (Table 6.1, p.118–119).
- Tekshiriladigan ko'rsatkichlar ro'yxati (Table 6.3, p.122–123):
  - diskriminatsiya: CAP/ROC, AR, AUROC, Gini*, FAR/HR/MR, KS, Pietra, CIER, IV, Brier;
  - kalibrlash: binomial test, Hosmer–Lemeshow, traffic light;
  - barqarorlik: PSI, HHI, PR, M1C/M2C, rating reversal.
- AR (Gini) 0 dan 1 gacha o'zgaradi. CAP egri chizig'i ideal va tasodifiy model egri chiziqlari orasida yotadi (p.134).
- AUROC tasodifiy modelda 0,5 ga, ideal modelda 1 ga teng. Bog'liqlik: **AR = 2·AUROC − 1** (p.135).
- Tuzatilgan Gini: **Gini* = AR·(1 − DR)**, bu yerda DR – tanlanmadagi defolt ulushi (p.136).
- Kontingensiya jadvali (Table 6.11, p.136): misolda I tur xatosi 30%, II tur xatosi (FAR) 20%. I tur xatosi qimmatroq, chunki asosiy qarz va foiz yo'qotiladi. Misolda HR = 70%, MR = 25% (Table 6.12, p.137).
- KS – good va bad taqsimotlarining kumulyativ funksiyalari orasidagi maksimal vertikal masofa, [0;1] oralig'ida (p.137).
- Kalibrlash testlari (p.139–142):
  - binomial test bir tomonlama. H0: "sinf PD si to'g'ri", H1: "PD past baholangan". Aktivlar korrelyatsiyasi bilan va korrelyatsiyasiz variantlari bor;
  - Hosmer–Lemeshow χ² butun portfel bo'yicha ham past, ham yuqori baholashni aniqlaydi;
  - traffic light: DR < PD_inf bo'lsa "yashil", DR > PD_sup bo'lsa "qizil", oradagi holat "sariq".
- 10 sinfli misol (Table 6.14, p.140): portfel DR = 4,67%. Masalan, 7-sinfda DR 8,70%, PD 7,49%; 10-sinfda DR 36,09%, PD 40,17%.
- PSI = Σ(P_i − C_i)·ln(P_i/C_i). Kichik qiymat barqarorlikni bildiradi (p.142–143).
- Rating reversal misoli: 3 yil davomida 87,6% barqaror, 12,4% teskari harakat (Table 6.15, p.143).

## 8.5–8.9 Stand-alone drayverlar va qo'llov (p.161–178)
- Mamlakat riski (p.161–163). Ishlatiladigan reyting turlari: CAR (country average rating), MRC (korporativ qarzdor uchun maksimal reyting) va suveren reyting. Stand-alone reyting MRC dan oshmasligi kerak.
- Sifat mezonlari (p.163–167): muhit va sektor; sektordagi pozitsiya (8 pog'onali shkala: "industry leader" dan "weak competitor" gacha); menejment (7 pog'ona: excellent → unacceptable); tashqi likvidlik manbalari (8 pog'ona).
- Moliyaviy tahlil 3 yillik o'rtachalar asosida olib boriladi (p.168). Agentlik koeffitsientlari keltiriladi (p.171).
- Qo'llovning umumiy tamoyillari (p.171–172): qo'llov global xususiyatga ega; yuridik kafolat qo'llov hisoblanmaydi (u GRR ga, ya'ni qaytarish darajasiga ta'sir qiladi); faqat bitta qo'llovchi subyekt hisobga olinadi.
- Qo'llovchi subyektni aniqlash mezonlari (p.173): strategik ahamiyat, brend, funding, ulush, track record, dividendlar va boshqalar.
- Salbiy qo'llov belgilari (p.174): nostandart transfer narxlar, yuqori royalti, fiskal integratsiyani suiiste'mol qilish, yuqoriga yo'naltirilgan moliyalash. Misol: qarz 10 mln, dividend 3 mln.
- Ijobiy qo'llovning kuchi va notch qoidalari (p.176):
  - absolyut qo'llov: yakuniy reyting qo'llovchi reytingiga teng;
  - juda kuchli: qo'llovchidan 1–2 notch past;
  - kuchli: 2–3 notch past;
  - kuchsiz: kamida 3 notch past.
- Salbiy qo'llov (p.176–177): juda kuchli bo'lsa, stand-alone dan kamida 2–3 notch past; kuchli bo'lsa 1–2 notch past.
- Yakuniy reyting (p.177–178): stand-alone reytingga qo'llov tuzatishi qo'shiladi. Sho'ba korxonalarni baholash "bottom-up" usulida bo'lishi kerak, "top-down" emas.

## Dissertatsiyada qo'llash
- §1.3/2.1 – Table 3.8 va 3.9 dagi bank rating modellari xalqaro amaliyotdagi og'irlik tuzilmasi namunasi sifatida keltiriladi. Ularni CAMELS va MBII bloklari bilan solishtirma jadvalga qo'yish mumkin. Rivojlanayotgan mamlakatlarda FX likvidlikning 30% og'irligi MBII dagi FX blokini asoslaydi.
- §3.1 – MBII og'irliklarini (0,2/0,3/0,25/0,15/0,1) ekspert asosida berishni p.76 dagi formulaga qarama-qarshi qo'yish mumkin: α_j = β_j/Σβ. Sezgirlik tahlili uchun 1–4 ball → 0–100 indeksni logit-regressiya bilan tekshirish taklif qilinadi.
- §4.1 (SIB) – Table 3.11 davlat qo'llovi matritsasini SQB va Mikrokreditbank uchun "stand-alone MBII" va "qo'llov bilan tuzatilgan MBII" ga ajratishda ishlatish mumkin (davlat ulushi >50% bo'lsa w = 100%).
- §4.1 – MBII ning bashorat kuchini baholash: AUROC, AR = 2AUROC−1, KS, Gini*, darajalar barqarorligi uchun PSI, 4 darajali shkala uchun o'tish matritsasi (PR, M1C).
- §4.2 – "Traffic light" mantig'ini ssenariy natijalarini rangli zonalarga ajratishda ishlatish mumkin.
- §1.3 – Basel III elementlari ro'yxati (p.5–6).

## Iqtiboslar
- "In any given loan portfolio, the higher the credit rating model accuracy ratio, the lower the regulatory capital required." (p.6)
- "For the evaluation of the bank counterparty credit risk, it is appropriate to develop a framework to adjust the PD estimated by the model according to implicit and explicit support given, in this case, by the parent company and/or the government." (p.77)
- "Governments also tend to support banks, especially when they are of high importance for the financial system of a country, providing them with implicit support." (p.87)
- "The AUROC and the AR parameters are linked by the relation: AR = 2 · AUROC − 1" (p.135)
- "Hence, model validation must be seen as an ongoing process" (p.114)
- "the small values of PSI are expressions of a good level of stability/representativeness ... high values are a symptom of instability." (p.143)
- "Legal guarantees are not support" (p.172)

# He Wei Ping «Banking Regulation in China» (Palgrave, 2014)

**2-bob (qisqa ko‘rib chiqildi, pp. 27–50)**
- Jamoat manfaatining ikki komponenti bor: "maqsad va natijalar" hamda "jarayon va protsedura". Global moliyaviy inqirozdan keyin (GFC) likvidlik jamoat ne’mati sifatida qaralmoqda (p. 29).
- Xususiy manfaat nazariyasining ikki tarmog‘i bor: siyosatchilar manfaati va tarmoq tomonidan "egallab olish" (capture) (p. 34).
- Regulyatsiya uch turga bo‘linadi: sistemli ("low-probability-high-impact risk", p. 37), prudensial va xulq-atvor (business conduct) regulyatsiyasi. Prudensial regulyatsiya kapital yetarliligi va risk-based yondashuvga asoslanadi. Uning asosiy xususiyati "to identify early warnings of risk" (p. 40).

**3-bob. Xitoyda bank regulyatsiyasi: nima, nega, qanday? (pp. 51–69)**
- 2003-yilda CBRC tashkil etilib, nazorat PBoC’dan ajratildi. Sabab: mikro (regulyativ) va makro (monetar) siyosat o‘rtasidagi manfaatlar to‘qnashuvi (p. 52).
- PBoC tizimli xavfsizlik uchun javobgar. Unda banklararo bozor, to‘lov tizimlari va kredit axboroti tizimi bor. Rasmiy depozit sug‘urtasi yo‘q, davlat yashirin kafolat beradi (p. 53).
- Prudensial sohalar (2003-yilgi Qonunning 21-moddasi): kapital yetarliligi, aktiv sifati, zaxira ajratish, risk konsentratsiyasi, bog‘liq tomonlar bilan bitimlar va likvidlik. Kredit/depozit nisbati ≤ 75%, 2011-yil iyundan boshlab har kuni monitoring qilinadi (p. 55).
- GFC’dan keyin makroprudensial choralar kiritildi: yuqoriroq kapital nisbati, leverage nisbati va kutilgan zarar bo‘yicha zaxira nisbati 2,5% (provisioning ratio) (p. 55).
- "Heavy touch" va "paternalistik" regulyatsiya. Masalan, direktor 11 holatda nomuvofiq deb topiladi, jumladan kengash yig‘ilishlarining ≥ 2/3 qismiga qatnashmasa (p. 56).
- Ob’ektga asoslangan yondashuv: PBoC tizimli regulyatsiyani, CBRC mikro va makro prudensial hamda xulq-atvor regulyatsiyasini olib boradi (p. 57).
- Jamoat manfaati maqsadlari: adolatli raqobat, xavfsizlik va mustahkamlik (safety and soundness), jamoatchilik ishonchi, omonatchilarni himoya qilish (p. 58). Xususiy manfaat ko‘rinishi: 4 trln yuanlik rag‘batlantirish paketining 2,82 trln yuanini mahalliy hokimiyatlar to‘plagan, bunda LGFP kreditlarining 80% i banklardan olingan (p. 59).
- Davlat yo‘naltirgan kreditlash uzoq muddatda barqarorlikka zarar yetkazadi (pp. 60–61). Uy-joy uchun minimal boshlang‘ich to‘lov: 90 m² gacha 20%, undan katta uylar uchun 30%, ikkinchi uy uchun 40%. 2013-yil iyunda repo stavkasi 25% gacha ko‘tarildi: regulyator barqarorlikni o‘sish uchun qurbon qilmaslik signalini bergan (pp. 61–62).
- To‘rt bosqichli huquqiy tizim: qonunlar → CBRC siyosati → yo‘riqnoma va qoidalar → "Window Guidance" (og‘zaki, yozma emas). Window Guidance CBRC’ning skoring tizimida katta vaznga ega (pp. 62–66).
- IRB 2007-yildan joriy qilingan. Bozor riski uchun standart yoki ichki model, operatsion risk uchun yondashuvni bank o‘zi tanlaydi (p. 64).
- Xulosa: "regulatory objectives are subordinated to the government’s economic and social policies" (p. 68).

**7-bob. Regulyatorni egallab olish – Regulatory Capture (pp. 131–147)**
- Capture ikki ko‘rinishda: hukumatlar tomonidan (milliy darajada siyosiy qaramlik, mahalliy darajada o‘zaro qaramlik) va regulyatsiya qilinuvchi banklar tomonidan (pp. 131–132).
- CBRC Davlat kengashiga hisobdor. Kadrlar va byudjet markazlashgan, natijada u "politicized banking regulator" hisoblanadi (pp. 133–134).
- Yashirin davlat kafolati bor ("banks are rescued before they fail"), shu sababli bozor intizomi zaif (p. 135).
- LGFP: ayrim kompaniyalarning qarzi mahalliy YaIMning taxminan 400% iga yetgan. 2009-yilda mahalliy hokimiyatlarning LGFP qarzi milliy YaIMning 23% ini tashkil etgan. "Entrusted loans" jami moliyalashning 10% dan ortig‘i, Shanxayda 15% ga teng va balansdan tashqari sistemli risk manbai (pp. 136–138).
- CBRC tuzilmasi: 36 viloyat byurosi, 300 kichik byuro, 1735 vakolatxona (p. 138).
- Xitoy banklari tomonidan capture: davlat egaligi, SASAC orqali kadrlar boshqaruvi va "revolving door" amaliyoti. CBRC paternalistik regulyator sifatida bank manfaatlarini ko‘zlaydi (pp. 140–141).
- Xorijiy banklar tomonidan capture: maslahat kengashi, so‘rovnomalar va ichki audit yo‘riqnomasi (2006) orqali amalga oshadi, natijada bilim mahalliy banklarga o‘tkaziladi (pp. 141–143).
- Xususiy sektor uchun oqibatlar: 2009-yilda korporativ kreditlarning faqat 22,2% i kichik biznesga berilgan. Chjeszyanda norasmiy moliyalash asosiy kapitalga investitsiyalarning 58% ini tashkil etgan. Mikrokredit kompaniyalarida bitta qarzdorga limit sof kapitalning ≤ 5% i (pp. 145–146).

**Dissertatsiyada qo‘llash**
- §1.1: Prudensial, sistemli va xulq-atvor regulyatsiyasi tasnifi (pp. 36–40) va "early warnings" tushunchasi barqarorlik turlarini ajratishda ishlatiladi.
- §3.2: Davlat banklari "fiskal siyosat organi" sifatida, yashirin kafolat va bozor intizomining zaifligi (p. 135). Bu SQB va Mikrokreditbank tahlilida, ayniqsa davlat yo‘naltirgan kreditlash riskini ko‘rsatishda qo‘llanadi.
- §3.1/4.1: Xitoyning L/D ≤ 75% va 2,5% zaxira (provisioning) nisbati MBII Likvidlik va Aktivlar bloklari uchun xalqaro qiyosiy chegara sifatida ishlatiladi. LGFP va "entrusted loans" esa balansdan tashqari risk bo‘yicha erta ogohlantirish signali uchun misol.
- §1.3/2.1: Window Guidance va CBRC skoringi regulyativ reyting modellarining milliy variantlariga misol bo‘ladi.

**Iqtiboslar**
- "In China, regulatory objectives are subordinated to the government’s economic and social policies." (p. 68)
- "It is safe to conclude that China has an effective implicit government guarantee scheme that banks are rescued before they fail." (p. 135)
- "The essential attribute of a risk-based regulatory theory is to identify early warnings of risk posed by banks." (p. 40)
- "a loan-to-deposit ratio requirement is a simple way to control loan size and to ensure adequate liquidity in Chinese banks." (p. 55)
- "Systemic risk is referred to as “low-probability-high-impact risk.”" (p. 37)

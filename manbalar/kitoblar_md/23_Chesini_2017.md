**2. Mazmun**

*4-bob. Crespi va Aliano (to‘liq)*
- Berger–DeYoung (1997) to‘rt gipotezasi (p.59–61):
  - **bad luck**: NPL o‘sishi samaradorlik pasayishidan oldin keladi;
  - **bad management**: past xarajat samaradorligi lag bilan NPL o‘sishiga olib keladi;
  - **skimping**: monitoringga tejash avval samaradorlikni oshiradi, keyin NPLni oshiradi (ishora teskari);
  - **moral hazard**: past kapital NPLni oshiradi.
- Podpiera–Weill (p.61): "kompensatsiyalangan risk" = NPL/kreditlar − foiz daromadi/kreditlar.
- Ma’lumotlar (p.63–67): Italiyaning 48 ta banki, 2010–2013. Ular sektor aktivlarining 82%, NPLning 66% i.
- Bank of Italy 272/2008 qoidasi bo‘yicha to‘rt toifa (p.64):
  - substandard (vaqtincha qiyinchilikdagi qarzdor);
  - >90 kun muddati o‘tgan;
  - bad loans (to‘lovga qodir emas);
  - restrukturizatsiya qilingan.
- Muammoli kreditlar/mijozlarga kreditlar: 8.49% dan 15.46% gacha. Kooperativ banklarda markaziy hududda 37.45% ga yetgan (Table 4.2, p.67).
- Model:
  - bog‘liq o‘zgaruvchi cq = umidsiz kreditlar/mijozlarga kreditlar (4.1), p.67;
  - pooled OLS, o‘zgaruvchilar t−1 da (4.2), p.68;
  - Arc = kreditlar/TA; Exp = muddati o‘tgan/kreditlar; Ris = restrukturizatsiya/kreditlar; Netinc = SFD/TA; Equity; Regional dummy; Efficiency = operatsion daromad/xarajat; Struct = foizsiz xarajatlar/(SFD + komissiya); IRB dummy (p.70–71).
- Natijalar (Table 4.4, p.72; R² 0.91, 138 kuzatuv):
  - Arc −12.2**; Exp +0.75***; Ris +0.42…0.51; Netinc −0.39**;
  - bad loans(−1) +1.12***: o‘zini o‘zi kuchaytiradi;
  - Regional +0.008**; Efficiency −0.0018…−0.0021*; Size −0.019*;
  - Equity va IRB ahamiyatsiz.
- VIF < 10 (Table 4.5, p.74). Sistemaviy GMM Exp va Ris natijalarini tasdiqlaydi (Table 4.7, p.74).
- Xulosa (p.75–76):
  - bad management gipotezasi tasdiqlandi: muddati o‘tgan va restrukturizatsiya qilingan kreditlar umidsiz kreditlarga aylanadi;
  - IRB bunga yordam bermagan;
  - Italiya 2015: umidsiz kreditlar 200 mlrd, jami NPL 338 mlrd yevro.

*2-bob (qisman). Cruz-García va boshq. (NIM)*
- Tanlanma va usul (p.11–21): 32 ta OECD mamlakati, 2003–2014, 54 540 kuzatuv. Ho–Saunders modeli kengaytirilgan va unga stavka hamda stavka kvadrati qo‘shilgan. System GMM (Arellano–Bond, Blundell–Bond).
- Natijalar (Table 2.2, p.25–26, [2]-ustun):
  - NIM(−1) 0.295; stavka 0.451**; stavka² −2.663**: munosabat konkav, maksimum 8.5% stavkada (p.24);
  - samaradorlik −0.008; ln(kreditlar) 0.278; risk aversiyasi 0.091; YaIM o‘sishi 0.204.
- Iqtisodiy ta’sir, 25-dan 75-persentilgacha o‘zgarish (Fig. 2.4, p.26–27): stavka +119 bp, hajm +83, risk aversiyasi +56, YaIM +51, samaradorlik −15 bp.
- 2008–2014 yillarda stavka pasayishi NIMni kamaytirgan (Table 2.3, p.28): yevro hududi −147 bp, AQSh −107, UK −158.
- Xulosa (p.29–30): past stavkalarda o‘sish va barqarorlik o‘rtasida trade-off bor. Stavkaning yanada pasayishi rentabellikni yemiradi.

*7-bob (qisman). Cosma va boshq. (biznes-model tasnifi)*
- Biznes-model ta’riflari, menejment adabiyotidagi uch oqim (p.133–139, Fig. 7.1): IKT va e-biznes; strategiya (korporativ "nima" va biznes "qanday"); strategik guruhlar (klaster).
- Bank biznes-modeli sxemasi (Fig. 7.2, p.140–141):
  - 1-daraja: korporativ strategiyalar – o‘sish, diversifikatsiya, xalqarolashuv, riskka munosabat;
  - SBA tarkibi va biznes-miks: aktiv/passiv va daromad tuzilmasi;
  - 2-daraja: biznes strategiyalar – daromad, samaradorlik, risk;
  - kontekst: makro, raqobat, regulyatsiya;
  - natija: rentabellik, risk, bozor qiymati.
- Adabiyotning birinchi oqimi – klaster yondashuvi (p.141–144):
  - Ayadi–de Groen: 6 indikator, 4 guruh;
  - Roengpitya (BIS): retail-funded, wholesale-funded, trading. 2007-yildagi wholesale va trading banklarning 2/5 qismi 2013-yilga kelib retailga o‘tgan;
  - ECB 2016: biznes-modellar "yopishqoq", kam o‘zgaradi;
  - De Meo: retail, diversified, investment, EBA mezonlari bilan 8 guruh;
  - Mergaert–Vennet: retail banklar rentabelroq va barqarorroq;
  - Bonaccorsi: risk zichligi = RWA/ekspozitsiya.
- Ikkinchi oqim – keng ta’rif, risk determinantlari (p.144–147): Altunbas 2011; Köhler (Z-score, foizsiz daromad, nodepozit funding); ECB 2016 (143 bank, Z-score); Van Oordt–Zhou (tizimli risk).
- Nazoratda biznes-model tahlili (BMA), SREPning 1-elementi, EBA 2014 (p.153–154):
  - faoliyatni identifikatsiya qilish;
  - biznes muhit;
  - kelajakka qaratilgan strategiya;
  - hayotiylik (1 yil), barqarorlik (3 yil), sikl bo‘yicha barqarorlik (>3 yil);
  - zaif nuqtalar.
  - Natijada banklar 4 sinfga va "FOLTF" toifasiga ajratiladi (p.155).
- Asosiy xulosalar (p.159–161): chakana funding eng muhim biznes-miks omili. Inqirozning birinchi to‘lqinida eng bardoshli bo‘lgan model (SME retail) keyingi davrda eng yomon natija ko‘rsatgan.

**3. Dissertatsiyada qo‘llash**
- §3.3 – Crespi–Aliano spetsifikatsiyasi NPL sun’iy yo‘ldosh modeli uchun:
  - O‘zbekistonda MB tasnifidagi "substandart", "shubhali" va "umidsiz" toifalar bilan lagli o‘tish (Exp, Ris → bad);
  - Efficiency va Struct indikatorlari bad management proksisi;
  - bad loans(t−1) autoregressiyasi GMM uchun asos.
- §4.2 – NPL o‘tish koeffitsiyenti (~0.75) ssenariy prognozida NPL migratsiyasi uchun analog bo‘ladi: stress ssenariyda substandart kreditlarning umidsizga o‘tishi.
- §3.1/4.2 – Cruz-Garcíaning konkav "stavka → NIM" bog‘liqligi rentabellik blokini (NIM) MB asosiy stavkasi ssenariylariga bog‘lash va optimistik ssenariyda stavka pasayishi NIMni yemirishini ko‘rsatish uchun.
- §4.1/3.2 – EBA BMA (1, 3 va >3 yil) va peer-tahlil tizimli muhim davlat banklari (SQB, Mikrokreditbank) uchun MBII-Basel qoidalarida biznes-model hayotiyligi bo‘yicha qo‘shimcha signal.
- §1.3/2.1 – biznes-modellar tipologiyasi (retail, wholesale, trading) MBII bo‘yicha reytingda peer guruhlarni ajratish uchun.

**4. Iqtiboslar**
- "problematic loans (past due and restructured) tend to transform into bad loans, demonstrating that credit managers were not able to implement recovery strategies" (p.75).
- "under the 'bad management' hypothesis, low cost efficiency Granger-causes larger amounts of problem loans" (p.59–60).
- "it is rather surprising that the use of IRB models is not significant in reducing NPLs during time" (p.75).
- "a change in interest rates has a greater impact on the net interest margin the lower the level of interest rates" (p.24).
- "there is a trade-off between economic growth and financial stability associated with the impact of expansionary monetary policy when the level of interest rates is very low" (p.30).
- "The BM most resilient in the first wave of the financial crisis (retail banks with loans oriented to SMEs) has been the worst performer in the subsequent economic slowdown" (p.160).
- "assessment of the BM's viability (within one year), sustainability (within three years) and sustainability over the cycle (more than three years)" (p.154).

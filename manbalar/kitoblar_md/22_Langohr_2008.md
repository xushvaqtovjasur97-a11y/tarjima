# Langohr H., Langohr P. «The Rating Agencies and Their Credit Ratings» (Wiley, 2008)

**Bibliografiya:** Langohr H., Langohr P. *The Rating Agencies and Their Credit Ratings: What They Are, How They Work and Why They Are Relevant.* – Chichester: John Wiley & Sons (Wiley Finance), 2008. – xxx + 484 p.

## 2.2 Kredit reyting shkalalari (p.40–74)
- Issuer rating va instrument rating ajratiladi. Instrument reytingi PD va LGD ni birlashtiradi (p.42).
- Uch xil "shkala" mavjud: fundamental ordinal shkala, bozor kredit spredlari va bozordan kelib chiqqan (market-implied) kardinal shkala (p.43).
- Reyting ordinal xarakterga ega: BB ning A dan xavfliroq ekanini bildiradi, lekin qanchalik xavfliroq ekanini ko'rsatmaydi (p.44).
- Uzoq muddatli reyting gorizonti 3–5 yil (p.44).
- 1980–2004 yillarda o'rtacha yillik defolt darajasi: investitsion daraja (BBB va yuqori) uchun 0,07% (maksimum 0,49%), spekulyativ daraja uchun 3,85% (maksimum 10,59%) (p.44–45).
- S&P shkalasi tavsiflari AAA dan D gacha (Exhibit 2.2, p.45).
- S&P, Fitch va Moody's notch larining 1–21(24) raqamli ekvivalentlari va Moody's KMV EDF bilan mosligi (Exhibit 2.3, p.47). Misollar: AAA 0,02%; A 0,32%; BBB 0,70%; BB 1,48%; B 4,14%; CCC 17%; CC/C 20%.
- Moody's 5 yillik kumulyativ defolt darajalari (1983–2006): Aaa 0,18%; Aa2 0,28%; Baa2 2,11%; Ba2 8,82%; B2 31,24%. Defoltlar shkala bo'ylab pastga qarab eksponensial o'sadi (Fig. 2.8, p.48).
- Moody's tadqiqoti: BB uchun PD BBB ga nisbatan 6 baravar yuqori (p.48, izoh 56).
- Qisqa muddatli shkalalar (A-1+/F1+/P-1 ... D) va uzoq muddatli reyting bilan moslik (Exhibit 2.4–2.5, p.51–52).
- Split ratings (p.55): S&P va Moody's reytinglari korrelyatsiyasi 0,940, lekin 40% hollarda asosiy kategoriya darajasida farq qiladi.
- Spredlar va reyting (Table 2.4–2.6, p.66–70):
  - bog'liqlik eksponensial;
  - 1–3 yillik muddatda AAA 49,5 bp, BBB 169 bp, B 761 bp;
  - spekulyativ darajada spred egri chizig'i teskari.
- Expected loss misoli: PD 43% va qaytarish 51% bo'lsa, EL ≈ 22% (p.67).
- Ordinal reyting shkalasini EDF ga geometrik interpolyatsiya bilan bog'lash. Bunda monotonlik va qavariqlik talab qilinadi (p.73).

## 6.3.2 Moliyaviy barqarorlik reytinglari (p.291–296)
- Bank balansi o'ziga xos: kapital aktivlarning 4–8% i, mijozlar oldidagi majburiyatlar 85–95% (p.291).
- Bank FSR quyidagilarni baholaydi (p.291): "xom ashyo" (aktivlar) sifati; xarajatlardan keyingi marja; kapital buferining yetarliligi; regulyativ talablarga muvofiqlik va ulardan ortiq zaxira.
- Banklar boshqa sohalarga nisbatan shaffof emas. Agentliklar reytinglari banklar bo'yicha ko'proq farq qiladi, ayniqsa kapital past va kreditlar ulushi yuqori bo'lganda (Morgan, 2002; p.292).
- Fitch bo'yicha bank "failure" hisoblanadi, agar u (p.292–293): davlat yoki depozit sug'urta fondi qo'llovi bilan, boshqa subyektga qo'shilish orqali, aksiyadorlarning yangi in'ektsiyasi bilan saqlanib qolsa yoki defolt qilsa.
- Failure darajasi defolt darajasidan yuqori. Ular orasidagi farq qo'llov hisobiga yuzaga keladi (p.293).
- FSR ta'rifi: tashqi qo'llovsiz stand-alone kuch, ya'ni bankka qo'llov kerak bo'lish ehtimoli o'lchovi (p.293).
- Tahlil ikki qismdan iborat (p.293–294): biznes profili (mamlakat riski, nazorat sifati, bozor pozitsiyasi, diversifikatsiya) va moliyaviy profil (CAMEL). CAMEL ichida aktivlar sifati eng muhim qism hisoblanadi.
- FSR + qo'llov ehtimoli → defolt ehtimoli → emitent reytingi. Valyuta "potolog"i (ceiling) qo'llanadi (p.294).
- Moody's BFSR shkalasi A–E (+/− modifikatorlar bilan). Fitch individual reytingi A–E (A/B ... D/E) va qo'shimcha "F" darajasi, ya'ni qo'llovsiz defolt qilgan bo'lardi (Exhibit 6.13, p.295).

## 7.2 Reyting aniqligi va barqarorligi (p.332–356)
- Aniqlik va barqarorlik bir-biriga zid xususiyatlar. I tur xatosi – defoltni o'tkazib yuborish; II tur – yolg'on signal (p.333–334).
- Moody's ko'rsatkichlari (Table 7.6, p.334):
  - 1 yillik AR: 2007-yil sentabrda 93,0%, tarixiy o'rtacha 84,5%;
  - 5 yillik AR: tarixiy o'rtacha 71,3%;
  - defoltdan oldingi o'rtacha reyting B2;
  - rating action rate 21,4%, large action 4,8%.
- Yillik defolt darajalari o'rtachasi, 1970–2006 (Table 7.7, p.335–336): barcha korporativ emitentlar 1,23%; Aaa 0; Baa 0,18%; Ba 1,15%; B 5,98%; Caa–C 23,83%. Variatsiya koeffitsienti taxminan 1.
- Kogorta usuli: marjinal defolt d_y^z(t) = x(t)/n(t). Kumulyativ defolt darajasi shu asosda hisoblanadi (p.337).
- Bir xil reytingdagi kogortalar sikl bo'yicha farq qiladi: Baa 5 yillik defolt 1977 yilda 0,57%, 1986 yilda 5,26% (p.338).
- Lorenz/CAP egri chizig'i, AR = (Lorenz va tasodifiy chiziq orasidagi yuza) / (ideal va tasodifiy chiziq orasidagi yuza) (p.339–340).
- S&P Gini koeffitsientlari (Table 7.8, p.341): 1 yil – 83%, 3 yil – 77%, 5 yil – 74%, 7 yil – 71%.
- S&P 1 yillik o'tish matritsasi (Table 7.9, p.342): AAA→AAA 88,34%; BBB→BBB 84,17%; BB→D 1,07%; B→D 4,99%; CCC/C→D 26,29%.
  - Diagonal elementlar barqarorlik darajasini bildiradi;
  - 1 − diagonal = rating action rate;
  - "D" ustuni PD ni ko'rsatadi (p.342).
- Barqarorlik o'lchovlari ta'riflari (p.343, 351): large action rate; reversal rate; drift = weighted upgrade − weighted downgrade; volatility = upgrade + downgrade; stability rate = 1 − (down + up).
- Mortality rates (Altman): CCC uchun 10 yillik kumulyativ 59,36%, AAA uchun 0,09% (Table 7.12, p.348).
- Ogohlantiruvchi signal sifati (p.353): defoltdan 48 oy oldin mediana B1, 17 oy oldin B2, defolt paytida Caa2. Defolt yaqinlashgan sari pasayish tezlashadi.
- "Rating momentum": downgrade dan keyin yana downgrade bo'lish ehtimoli upgrade dan 9 baravar yuqori (p.354).
- Siklga bog'liqlik (p.354–355): retsessiyada upgrade ulushi pasayadi (2000 yilda 48%, 2002 yilda 19%). Yangi reytinglar protsiklik. TTC barqarorroq, lekin aniqligi pastroq.

## Dissertatsiyada qo'llash
- §1.1/1.3/2.1 – Fitch ning bank "failure" ta'rifi va "failure ≠ default" farqi bank moliyaviy barqarorligi kategoriyasini aniqlashda nazariy tayanch bo'ladi. Barqarorlik o'lchovi qo'llovsiz stand-alone kuch sifatida talqin qilinadi.
- §3.1 – MBII ning 4 darajasi (75/50/25) Moody's BFSR A–E shkalasiga va CAMEL tuzilishiga mos keladi. Ekspert verbal tavsiflarni Exhibit 6.13 asosida yozish mumkin.
- §4.1 – SIB lar uchun MBII "stand-alone" ko'rsatkich sifatida, davlat qo'llovi esa alohida (FSR → issuer rating mantig'i bilan).
- §4.1 – Gini/AR, CAP, 1 yillik o'tish matritsasi, stability rate va drift ni MBII darajalari uchun hisoblash. Gorizont uzaygan sari AR pasayishini kutish kerak (83→71%).
- §4.2 – Barqarorlik va aniqlik o'rtasidagi kelishuv hamda TTC/PIT farqi MBII prognozini talqin qilishda; protsiklik ta'sir stress-ssenariyda.
- §1.3/2.1 – Exhibit 2.3 reytingni PD ga o'tkazish jadvali sifatida (MBII ni ordinal shkala sifatida tushuntirishda).

## Iqtiboslar
- "The financial strength rating can be understood as a measure of the likelihood that a bank will require support." (p.293)
- "The failure rate of banks is substantially higher than their default rate, and this difference is support, typically provided by governments." (p.293)
- "Asset quality – This is the most important part of bank analysis as it is the main driver of future earnings." (p.294)
- "It is an index, not an absolute measure." (p.44)
- "Accuracy and stability are two inherently conflicting characteristics of ratings." (p.334)
- "Diagonal elements are a direct measure of stability" (p.342)
- "ratings need to be sufficiently cycle neutral to bring stability, but also sufficiently timely to bring enough accuracy" (p.355)

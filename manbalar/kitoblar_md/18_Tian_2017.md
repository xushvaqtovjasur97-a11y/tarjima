# Tian W. (ed.) «Commercial Banking Risk Management» (Palgrave, 2017)

Boblar: Tian W. «Regulatory Capital Requirement in Basel III» (pp. 3–34); Li L. «Liquidity Risk» (pp. 103–120); Zhu S.H. «Region and Sector Effects in Stress Testing of Commercial Loan Portfolio» (pp. 201–230); Todd B.A., Gardner D.T., Omer V. «Estimating the Impact of Model Limitations in Capital Stress Testing» (pp. 231–250).
Kitobdagi xatolar: jami kapital 10,5% ga «January 2009» deb yozilgan (to‘g‘risi 2019, p. 7); CCyB joriy etilishi «2006–2008» (to‘g‘risi 2016–2018, p. 24).

## Tian: Bazel III da regulyativ kapital (pp. 3–34)
- Bazel III «risk sensitive»dan «capital intensive»ga siljish (p. 3).
- 4 yangilik: CET1 2% → 4,5%, CCB 2,5%, CCyB 0–2,5%; stressed VaR, CVA; leverage ratio; LCR (2015) va NSFR (2018) (pp. 6–7).
- CET1 ga 14 mezon; ayiriladigan moddalar: gudvil, kelajak foydaga bog‘liq DTA, zaxiralarning EL dan kam qismi va boshqalar; ahamiyatli investitsiyalar, MSR va DTA har biri ≤10% CET1, jami ≤15% (pp. 9–10).
- Tier 2: muddat ≥5 yil; umumiy zaxiralar standart yondashuvda ≤1,25% kredit RWA (pp. 11–12).
- RWA = kredit RWA + 12,5 × operatsion yuk + 12,5 × bozor yuki (p. 13). Minimumlar: CET1 ≥4,5%, Tier 1 ≥6%, jami ≥8% (p. 15).
- Leverage = Tier 1 / Total exposure ≥3% (pp. 15–16).
- CCB: foydani saqlash shkalasi CET1 bo‘yicha 4,5–5,125% → 100%; 5,125–5,75% → 80%; 5,75–6,375% → 60%; 6,375–7% → 40%; >7% → 0% (pp. 20–22).
- G-SIB: 5 kategoriya × 20% (hajm, o‘zaro bog‘liqlik, o‘rnini bosish qiyinligi, transchegaraviy faoliyat, murakkablik); qo‘shimcha CET1 1 / 1,5 / 2 / 2,5%, bo‘sh bucket 3,5% (pp. 24–28).
- TLAC: 2019-yildan ≥16% RWA, 2022-yildan ≥18% (p. 28).

## Li: Likvidlik riski (pp. 103–120)
- Boshqaruv sikli: identifikatsiya → limitlar → o‘lchash va hisobot → monitoring (pp. 107–109).
- 90 kunlik risk appetite: likvid aktivlar buferi / sof chiqim ≥ limit (masalan 100%) (pp. 109–110).
- LCR = HQLA / 30 kunlik sof chiqim; joriy etish 60% (2015), 70% (2016), 100% (2018). HQLA: L1 ≥60%; L2A ≤40%; L2B ≤15% (pp. 110–111).

## Zhu: Stress-testda hudud va tarmoq ta’siri (pp. 201–230)
- 4 qadam: o‘tish matritsasidan yagona «kredit indeksi» Z olinadi → Z makro o‘zgaruvchilarga regressiya qilinadi → prognoz → stress-matritsalar (p. 203).
- Vasicek/ASRF: PD(g|Z) = Φ[(Φ⁻¹(PD) − √ρ·Z)/√(1−ρ)] (pp. 205–207). Z<0 — yomon yil (p. 209).
- ρ: butun tanlanma 9–13%; AQSh 12%, EI 15%, rivojlanayotgan bozorlar 20%; ko‘chmas mulk 42,5% (pp. 210–212).
- CIndex = a + b₀·YaIM + b₁·CPI + b₂·ipoteka stavkasi + b₃·DJIA + b₄·uy narxi; R² 90%; indeks YaIMdan 2 chorak oldin harakatlanadi (pp. 212–214).
- EL = Σ EAD·PD·LGD; misol: C&I portfel bo‘yicha yo‘qotish 6,4%, CCAR 2013 medianasi 6,5% (pp. 224–226).

## Todd va boshq.: Modellar cheklovlarining ta’siri (pp. 231–250)
- «Model limitation buffer» — prognoz noaniqligi uchun qo‘shimcha kapital (p. 232).
- 4 manba: qoldiq xato (out-of-time backtest, 9 chorak), model tanlash noaniqligi, ishlab chiqish kamchiliklari, rejim o‘zgarishi (pp. 233–243).
- ΔCET1_9Q ≈ CET1_0·[(NetRev + ΔValue)/Capital_0 − ΔRWA/RWA_0] (pp. 244–246); agregatsiya Buffer = √(δᵀ·C·δ), mukammal korrelyatsiyada oddiy yig‘indi (pp. 246–247).
